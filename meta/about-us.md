# About Us

## The AI HEART Project
Formed in 2025, the AI HEART Project is a small group of independent researchers from various backgrounds who are interested in working to improve the safety of AI technology. The word HEART is an acronym standing for Human-Empathetic AI Research & Technology.

Contact us at aiheartproject@gmail.com.
